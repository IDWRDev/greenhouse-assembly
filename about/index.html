Welcome to our house

A place where truth takes root and lives learn to flourish.

The GreenHouse Assembly is being cultivated as a Christ-centred environment for deep faith, renewed minds, purposeful community and Kingdom influence.

Why a greenhouse?

Growth needs the right environment.

A greenhouse does not manufacture life. It creates the conditions in which life can take root, become resilient and bear fruit. Light is welcomed. Growth is tended. Every plant is given room to mature.

That picture shapes our understanding of ministry. We gather around Christ and His truth so people can be known, formed, strengthened and sent into the world with wisdom and purpose.

Our direction

Cultivating people who carry Kingdom life everywhere.

Our mission and vision hold personal formation and public responsibility together. We want truth to take root deeply enough that it becomes wisdom, character, service and life for others.

Our mission

Form people deeply.

We cultivate Christ-centred lives through biblical teaching, prayer, worship, wise community and practical service.

Our vision

Release life widely.

We envision a spiritually mature, intellectually grounded and creatively excellent people carrying Kingdom wisdom into every sphere of life.

Our formation architecture

From encounter to influence.

The GreenHouse journey connects personal transformation with faithful public responsibility.

  1. 01

    Know

    Truth gives growth its roots: Scripture, Gospel, covenant and the wisdom of God.

  2. 02

    Become

    Formation renews the mind, shapes character and brings the whole life under Christ.

  3. 03

    Belong

    Community gives growth a home through worship, friendship, honesty and care.

  4. 04

    Serve

    Maturity becomes contribution through gifts offered with humility and excellence.

  5. 05

    Influence

    Kingdom life travels into family, vocation, leadership, creativity and culture.

The centre of our faith

Christ at the centre.
Every part of life transformed.

Jesus Christ is the centre of our faith and the pattern of our life. We receive Scripture as God’s truth, depend on the Holy Spirit for renewal, and gather in prayer, worship and community so that belief becomes faithful practice.

We believe the Gospel reaches the whole person. It reconciles us to God, reshapes what we love, renews how we think and teaches us to live with holiness, mercy, courage and responsibility in the world.

Leadership

Formation before visibility.

Christian leadership begins with character, stewardship and service. At The GreenHouse, influence is a responsibility to protect people, tell the truth, welcome accountability and help others become mature—not a platform for self-importance.

Leadership information will be presented with the clarity needed for trust, responsible contact and public accountability as the ministry’s structures develop.

Our leadership standard
  • Character before platform
  • Service before status
  • Wisdom before speed
  • Stewardship before scale
