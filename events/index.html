Why we gather

Presence, truth, formation and family.

01

Worship

We turn our attention toward God with reverence, gratitude and joyful response. Worship is not performance; it is a people learning what deserves their allegiance.

02

Scripture

We receive Scripture as more than information. It names reality, renews understanding and gives ordinary life a faithful direction.

03

Prayer

We make room for communion, intercession and honest dependence. Prayer teaches us to bring life under God rather than ask God to bless our own agendas.

04

Community

We grow through relationships where people can be welcomed, known, encouraged and called forward in love.

Your first gathering

A clear way to join—and keep growing.

You do not need insider knowledge or a perfect spiritual history. Begin with an official channel, arrive with attention and let one faithful response carry the message beyond the screen.

  1. 01

    Choose an official channel

    Use one of the five verified profiles above rather than a copied or outdated link. Follow the platform you already use and enable notifications if you want gathering announcements to reach you directly.

    View the official channels ↑
  2. 02

    Check the latest gathering notice

    Open the ministry’s newest announcement for the current platform and time. If the details are unclear, ask the team to confirm them before the gathering begins.

  3. 03

    Prepare your attention

    Join a few minutes early, reduce distractions where possible, keep Scripture and something for notes nearby, and come ready to listen rather than merely consume.

  4. 04

    Participate with wisdom

    Use chat, comments or prayer responses respectfully where they are available. Protect personal information in public spaces, and use the private contact route when a question needs care or follow-up.

  5. 05

    Carry one truth into practice

    Write down one conviction, one question and one action. Continue through the teaching library or Reading Room, begin a conversation, or return for the next gathering. Formation continues after the stream ends.

The heart of welcome

You do not have to arrive finished.

A gathering can be a beginning: a place to encounter God, ask honest questions and find people who will walk with you toward a more faithful life.

We believe a healthy welcome is more than a friendly greeting. It is clarity without pressure, attention without intrusion and room for a person to be present without pretending.

Whether you have followed Christ for years, are returning after a long distance, or are simply curious, you are welcome to listen, receive and take the next step at a pace that is honest.

If you need a practical answer before coming, use the contact form. A clear question is a good place to begin.

Common questions

Helpful answers before you join.

We want your first online gathering to feel clear and unpressured. If your question is not answered here, send it to us and we will help.

Where are gatherings happening now?

Gatherings are online for now through the five official channels published on this page: YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X. Follow the platform that works best for you and check its latest GreenHouse announcement.

How do I find the next gathering time?

Follow an official profile and enable notifications for new announcements. Because schedules can change, use the contact form or email contact@greenhouseassembly.org when you need the ministry team to confirm the current time.

What can I expect online?

Expect a Christ-centred gathering shaped by worship, Scripture, prayer, teaching and a practical invitation to grow. You are free to listen, participate at an honest pace and contact the team with questions afterwards.

Can I join if I am new to church or returning after time away?

Yes. You do not need to understand every practice before joining. Come with your questions, receive what is helpful and take the next step that is honest for you.

Do I need a social-platform account?

Viewing and interaction requirements vary by platform. Public content may be viewable without signing in, while notifications, chat or comments may require an account. If one platform creates a barrier, try another official channel or ask the team for the clearest route.

How should I ask for prayer or personal follow-up?

Do not post sensitive personal information in a public comment or live chat. Use the private prayer and care enquiry so the ministry can respond through a more responsible route.

What if I need captions or another accessibility adjustment?

Platform features differ. Tell us what would help you participate well and we will explain what the current channel supports or help you find a clearer alternative.

Will in-person gatherings be announced here?

Yes. If a confirmed in-person gathering is introduced, its verified time, location, accessibility and practical details will be published clearly before anyone is asked to travel.
