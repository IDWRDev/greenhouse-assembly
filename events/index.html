Why we gather

Presence, truth, formation and family.

01

Worship

We turn our attention toward God with reverence, gratitude and joyful response. Worship is not performance; it is a people learning what deserves their allegiance.

02

Scripture

Your first gathering

A simple guide for joining online.

You do not need to know every person or pattern before you join. Begin with the verified link, settle into a space where you can pay attention and participate at an honest pace.

  1. 01

    Request the current link

    Use the contact form or email us for the next gathering time and verified social-platform link. We will publish permanent profile links here once they are supplied.

  2. 02

    Make room to be present

    Join a few minutes early, reduce distractions where possible and bring something for notes. Online does not have to mean distant or passive.

  3. 03

    Come with your questions

    You do not need to perform or pretend. Listen, participate at your own pace and note anything you would like to explore afterwards.

  4. 04

    Use the available conversation

    Where the platform provides chat or response, engage respectfully. You can also contact the team privately after the gathering for a clearer next step.

  5. 05

    Take one faithful step

    Another gathering, prayer, a question or exploring a ministry pathway can be enough. Growth is not rushed; it is practised.

The heart of welcome

You do not have to arrive finished.

A gathering can be a beginning: a place to encounter God, ask honest questions and find people who will walk with you toward a more faithful life.

We believe a healthy welcome is more than a friendly greeting. It is clarity without pressure, attention without intrusion and room for a person to be present without pretending.

Whether you have followed Christ for years, are returning after a long distance, or are simply curious, you are welcome to listen, receive and take the next step at a pace that is honest.

If you need a practical answer before coming, use the contact form. A clear question is a good place to begin.

Common questions

Helpful answers before you join.

We want your first online gathering to feel clear and unpressured. If your question is not answered here, send it to us and we will help.

Where are gatherings happening now?

Gatherings are online across our social platforms for now. We will add the approved profile links to this page when they are supplied.

How do I get the next live link and time?

Use the contact form or email contact@greenhouseassembly.org. Ask for the current gathering schedule and verified platform link so you do not have to rely on an old post.

What can I expect online?

Expect a gathering shaped by worship, Scripture, prayer and community. You are free to listen, participate at your own pace and contact the team with questions afterwards.

Can I join if I am new to church or returning after time away?

Yes. You do not need to know every person or practice before joining. Come with your questions and take the next step that is honest for you.

Do I need a social-platform account?

That can depend on the platform and the kind of gathering. Ask us for the current link and we will explain any sign-in or participation requirement.

What if I need captions or another accessibility adjustment?

Tell us what would help you participate well. We will share what the current platform supports and help you find the clearest available route.

Will in-person gatherings be announced here?

Yes. If a confirmed in-person gathering is introduced, its verified time, location and practical details will be published clearly before anyone is asked to travel.
