## Truth boundary

The new copy is an editorial articulation of themes already present in the repository and the approved master directive. It does not invent founders, leaders, ministry history, denominational identity, schedules, addresses, statistics, testimonies, attendance, publication details, prices, active programmes, media, giving methods, or contact routes.

Formal mission, vision, doctrine, history, and leadership language still requires owner review before it is treated as final institutional wording.
