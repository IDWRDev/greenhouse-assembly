## Visual meaning

- The house outline represents belonging, covering, community, and a place intentionally shaped for growth.
- The seedling represents formation, renewal, potential, rootedness, and multiplication.
- The descending light represents truth, revelation, divine presence, and the conditions that make growth possible.
- Deep green is the primary institutional colour; warm gold is an accent for light, wisdom, value, and cultivated excellence.

## Usage rules

- Keep clear space around the symbol; do not crowd it with text or controls.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, outline, or add effects to the logo.
- Use the navigation mark when the full name is already present in adjacent HTML text.
- Use meaningful HTML text for the ministry name; do not rely on pixels for essential identity copy.
- Prefer the full primary lockup on light surfaces and the footer lockup on dark green.
- Preserve the favicon set as generated derivatives of the navigation mark.
- Treat `assets/brand/`, `assets/images/`, and `assets/images/roll up design/` as source/archive material, not direct web-delivery folders.

## Archive and duplication notes

Several files are byte-identical duplicates:

- `assets/brand/green-logo.png` and `assets/images/green-logo.png`
- `assets/brand/white-logo.png`, `assets/images/white-logo.png`, and `assets/images/greenhouse-logo.png`
- `assets/brand/white-logo2.png`, `assets/images/white-logo2.png`, and `assets/logos/IMG_20260709_222236_965.png`
- Each of the three roll-up designs is duplicated between `assets/brand/` and `assets/images/roll up design/`

The oversized source artwork has deliberately not been deleted in Milestone 1. A later media-production milestone should move archive-only originals out of the public delivery tree after ownership and downstream usage are confirmed.
