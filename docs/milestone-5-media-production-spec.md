# Milestone 5 - Original Media Production Specification

## Art direction

The visual system is called **Cultivated Intelligence**: documentary warmth, architectural discipline, Nigerian and Black-global dignity, botanical life, deep thought, and luminous spiritual atmosphere.

The work should feel contemporary and globally credible without becoming anonymous luxury-church imagery. People should appear thoughtful, grounded, expressive, and fully human. Environments should suggest formation: libraries, tables, gardens, greenhouse architecture, studios, homes, and calm gathering spaces. Nigerian and Black-global identity grounds the visual system, while multi-person community scenes should naturally include people of different races and backgrounds to communicate a genuinely global welcome.

## Visual constants

- Deep forest green, muted leaf green, warm gold, cream, natural wood, stone, linen, and parchment.
- Natural or motivated cinematic light; no artificial “heaven rays” overpowering the people.
- Documentary realism with natural skin texture, credible hands, realistic books, and lived-in environments.
- Nigerian and Black-global representation without costume, poverty, prosperity, or worship stereotypes; group scenes should include natural multiracial representation where appropriate.
- Negative space is planned for HTML copy; generated lettering is prohibited.
- No invented historical documentation, visible brands, watermarks, celebrity likenesses, fake crowds, or unsupported ministry claims.

## Production prompts

### Architecture of Redemption

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Asset type: premium 16:9 website teaching-series image.

Primary request: A contemporary Black Nigerian biblical scholar in a refined, warm library-studio, studying an open Bible beside restrained archival-style maps and handwritten notes. The person is thoughtful rather than posing, dressed in understated modern clothing. The environment combines dark wood, cream paper, deep green upholstery, one living plant, and subtle architectural lines that suggest coherence and construction.

Composition: wide cinematic frame, scholar and study table weighted to the right half, generous shadowed negative space on the left for HTML typography, eye-level 35mm documentary lens.

Lighting: warm directional window light with soft gold highlights and deep green shadows; intelligent, calm, rigorous, hopeful.

Constraints: realistic hands and book pages, dignified contemporary African representation, no legible generated text, no logos, no watermark, no pulpit, no robe, no theatrical worship pose, no fake ancient artefacts.

### Spiritual Formation

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Asset type: premium 16:9 teaching-pathway image.

Primary request: A Black Nigerian woman in a quiet contemporary home reading Scripture and writing in a journal at first light, with natural plants and soft linen textures. The moment feels attentive, disciplined and peaceful rather than staged.

Composition: subject left-centre, window and calm negative space to the right, 50mm natural documentary framing.

Lighting: early morning natural light, warm cream, muted gold and forest green colour grade.

Constraints: natural hands, realistic pages, no visible generated writing, no watermark, no exaggerated prayer pose, no fantasy glow.

### Belonging

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Asset type: premium 3:2 community image.

Primary request: A small multigenerational Black Nigerian and diaspora group sharing a thoughtful, joyful conversation around a long wooden table in a light-filled greenhouse-inspired community room. Real expressions, varied ages, contemporary clothing, tea and notebooks, plants and warm architecture.

Composition: candid medium-wide documentary frame; preserve faces within the central 60 percent for mobile crops.

Constraints: no one looking at camera, no staged cheering, no giant crowd, no visible logos or generated text, no stereotypes.

### Creative Dominion

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Asset type: premium 16:9 leadership and creativity image.

Primary request: Black Nigerian creative professionals collaborating in a contemporary design and media studio, reviewing physical sketches, storyboards and material samples around a table. The scene communicates purpose, craft, leadership and responsible creation.

Composition: wide documentary frame with group weighted right and lower centre, safe negative space upper left.

Constraints: no readable screen interfaces, no logos, no generated lettering, no corporate-stock handshakes, no exaggerated luxury.

## Accessibility and delivery

- Decorative atmosphere is represented with empty alt text; meaningful scenes receive concise descriptions that communicate purpose, not race alone.
- HTML headings and labels carry all essential meaning; no generated image contains required text.
- Final delivery should include responsive AVIF/WebP derivatives after the selected PNG masters are approved.
- Major below-fold imagery should use `loading="lazy"` and explicit dimensions or aspect ratios.
- Social images are decorative metadata and do not replace the page title or description.

## Provenance record

Every generated file must record generation date, final prompt, tool mode, intended use, dimensions, review state, and replacement priority in `docs/media-register.md` before publication.
