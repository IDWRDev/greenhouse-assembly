Resources/Reading Room/Purpose, Faithfulness and Responsibility
The GreenHouse Assembly / Reading Room

Reading plan · 6 minute read

Purpose grows where faithfulness meets responsibility.

Calling is less about becoming exceptional and more about becoming available for the good work God places in front of us.

Questions about calling can become heavy when we imagine there is one hidden decision that will either unlock or ruin our future. Scripture offers a steadier picture. God calls people into relationship and sends them into useful service, often through ordinary responsibilities, developed skill, faithful character and a growing love for the needs around them.

That does not make discernment easy. We still need prayer, counsel, courage and patience. But it frees us from waiting for a dramatic feeling before we act. A calling can take shape as we learn a craft, keep a promise, care for a neighbour, complete difficult work well or return to a responsibility we would rather avoid.

Discern with a whole life

Consider four questions together. What gifts or capacities have been entrusted to you? What needs can you see clearly? What responsibilities are already yours? What kind of person will this work require you to become? Good discernment does not ignore desire, but it tests desire by love, truth, community and the actual demands of service.

Take the next faithful step without demanding that it explain your whole future. Clarity often arrives while a person is already walking responsibly.

Map the next step

Read Colossians 3:12–17. Write one responsibility, one capacity to develop and one person who can help you discern with wisdom.

Continue your formation

Read slowly. Practise faithfully.
