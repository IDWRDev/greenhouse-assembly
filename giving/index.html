Giving questions

Clarity before commitment.

Which payment methods are available?

When enabled, Paystack checkout can present approved card, bank and local payment options. Crypto gifts are handled through a separate Cryptomus invoice.

Can I make a recurring gift?

Recurring giving will be made available only after the ministry has approved the provider setup, terms and donor communication for that option.

Will my card details be stored by GreenHouse?

No. Card details are entered on the payment provider’s secure checkout and are not collected or stored by this website.

What if a payment appears unsuccessful?

Do not retry repeatedly. Keep the provider reference, check your bank or wallet activity, then contact the ministry through the official contact page.
