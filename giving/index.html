A life of generosity

Give with gratitude, clarity and care.

Generosity is an expression of worship, freedom and faithful stewardship—not pressure. We believe every invitation to give should be clear about its purpose and safe in its method.

Why we give

Gratitude that becomes
faithful action.

Christian generosity begins with gratitude to God. It trains the heart away from fear and self-importance, and toward trust, responsibility and care for others.

We want every giving experience to be voluntary, secure, clearly explained and handled with appropriate accountability.

Our stewardship commitments

Trust deserves responsible systems.

01

No pressure

Giving should never be manipulated through fear, shame or inflated promises.

02

Secure methods

Funds should be sent only through approved, tested and clearly identified channels.

03

Clear purpose

Public giving language should explain its intended ministry context without unsupported claims.

04

Wise care

Personal banking passwords, card PINs and sensitive credentials should never be requested.

What stewardship serves

Generosity that helps life grow.

Our stewardship vision supports the work of forming people, strengthening generations and serving communities. Any specific appeal should name its purpose clearly before anyone is invited to participate.

Giving safely

Use only a confirmed GreenHouse giving channel.

There is no public online payment link on this website at present. When giving opens, the secure route will be published on this page and clearly identified through the ministry’s official channels.

Protect yourself from fraud.

Do not send funds using payment details copied from forwarded messages, unofficial accounts or unfamiliar links. If you are uncertain, contact the ministry before taking action.

Giving questions

Clarity before commitment.

Responsible generosity begins with truthful information and the freedom to ask questions.

Can I give online now?

Not at present. This page does not currently contain an active payment link, bank account or giving form. Please do not treat details received elsewhere as official unless the ministry confirms them.

How will I recognise the official giving route?

It will be published here on greenhouseassembly.org, use a secure HTTPS address and clearly identify the organisation receiving the payment. Important terms, available payment methods and confirmation steps will be explained before you proceed.

What about recurring or international giving?

Those options will be described only when an official provider supports them safely. No one should feel pressured to use a method that is unclear, inaccessible or unsuitable for their circumstances.

How can I ask a question or verify a request?

Use the contact form or email contact@greenhouseassembly.org. Never send banking passwords, card PINs, one-time codes or financial credentials.

Stewardship with wisdom

Give freely.
Give responsibly.

Generosity should be informed, voluntary and secure. Until an official giving channel appears here, contact us with questions and do not rely on payment details from another source.
