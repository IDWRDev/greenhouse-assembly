Flagship book

The Divine Art of Violence

The Lawful Force of Becoming — a theological reflection on transformation, governed strength and creative responsibility.

Good Idawari examines the difficult work of leaving false patterns behind: the structures that once protected us, the habits that no longer serve truth and the forms of power that must answer to love, restraint and consequence.

This is not a celebration of harm, domination or impulse. It is an invitation to pursue clarity, disciplined responsibility and a life capable of carrying weight without losing its humanity.
