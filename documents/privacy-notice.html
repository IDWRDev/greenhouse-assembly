Privacy notice

Last updated: 12 August 2026. This notice explains how The GreenHouse Assembly Ministries handles information submitted through this website.

Information you choose to provide

The contact form collects your name, email address, optional phone number, enquiry category and message. Please do not submit passwords, financial credentials, card details or extensive medical or safeguarding information.

How we use it

We use contact-form information to receive, route and respond to your enquiry, maintain responsible correspondence and protect the website from misuse. The form sends submissions to the ministry team at info@greenhouseassembly.org through a third-party form-processing service. The public address for direct contact is contact@greenhouseassembly.org.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared only with service providers needed to transmit the form and with ministry team members responsible for the enquiry. It should be kept only as long as reasonably needed for correspondence, record-keeping, safety or legal obligations, then removed or anonymised where appropriate.

Your choices

You may choose to email us instead of using the form. You may also ask what personal information you have submitted, request a correction or ask for deletion where there is no overriding responsibility to retain it.

External platforms and links

Links to social networks, video services or other websites are governed by those services’ own privacy practices. Review their notices before submitting information to them.

Questions

For a privacy question, contact contact@greenhouseassembly.org and use the subject “Privacy enquiry.”
