Accessibility

Last updated: 14 August 2026. The GreenHouse Assembly Ministries is committed to making this website and its online resources usable by as many people as possible.

What we have built in

The website uses responsive layouts, semantic headings, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, descriptive alternative text where images carry meaning, labelled forms, skip links and reduced-motion support. Text and controls are designed to remain usable across common mobile, tablet and desktop widths.

Documents and media

Downloadable guides are designed with readable structure and contrast. Videos should clearly identify whether they contain speech or are silent. If a resource is difficult to use with your assistive technology, ask us for help or an alternative format.

External platforms

Our official YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X channels are operated through third-party services. Their captions, keyboard support, contrast and other accessibility features are controlled by each provider and may differ from this website. If one channel creates a barrier, contact us and we will help you identify another available route.

Getting help

Email contact@greenhouseassembly.org with the page or resource you were using, the difficulty you encountered and, if helpful, your browser or assistive technology. We will use that information to investigate the problem and improve the experience.
