Your first visit

A simple guide for arriving well.

  1. 01

    Confirm before travelling

    Check this page or a verified ministry channel for the current time, venue and any registration requirement.

  2. 02

    Come as you are

    You do not need to perform or pretend. Arrive ready to listen, participate at your own pace and meet people naturally.

  3. 03

    Ask for what you need

    Family, mobility, sensory and other accessibility guidance should be confirmed before your visit when support is important.

  4. 04

    Take one next step

    A conversation, another visit, prayer or exploring a ministry pathway can be enough. There is no pressure to rush.

Common questions

Helpful answers before you arrive.

When and where do you meet?

Verified times and venue information are still required before publication.

Can first-time visitors attend?

Yes. The intended experience is welcoming and accessible to people exploring at their own pace.

What should I wear?

Come in clothing that is comfortable and appropriate for a shared public gathering.

Are children’s arrangements available?

Family arrangements and safeguarding information must be confirmed before they are represented as active.

Do I need to register?

No registration should be assumed unless a specific verified gathering says otherwise.
