Your first visit

A simple guide for arriving well.

You do not need to know every song, person or pattern before you arrive. Begin with the next clear step and give yourself room to take in the experience.

  1. 01

    Confirm before travelling

    Use the contact form for the current time, venue, transport guidance and any registration requirement. We would rather give you a clear answer than leave you guessing.

  2. 02

    Come with your questions

    You do not need to perform or pretend. Arrive ready to listen, participate at your own pace and take in what is useful for your next step.

  3. 03

    Ask for what you need

    If family, mobility, sensory or other practical support matters to your visit, let us know beforehand so we can respond with care.

  4. 04

    Stay for one conversation

    If you are comfortable, meet someone after the gathering. A short conversation can help you understand the house and decide what a wise next step could be.

  5. 05

    Take one faithful step

    Another visit, prayer, a question or exploring a ministry pathway can be enough. Growth is not rushed; it is practised.

The heart of welcome

You do not have to arrive finished.

A gathering can be a beginning: a place to encounter God, ask honest questions and find people who will walk with you toward a more faithful life.

We believe a healthy welcome is more than a friendly greeting. It is clarity without pressure, attention without intrusion and room for a person to be present without pretending.

Whether you have followed Christ for years, are returning after a long distance, or are simply curious, you are welcome to listen, receive and take the next step at a pace that is honest.

If you need a practical answer before coming, use the contact form. A clear question is a good place to begin.

Common questions

Helpful answers before you arrive.

When and where do you meet?

Please ask us for the most current confirmed time and venue before travelling. We do not want anyone relying on an out-of-date listing.

Can first-time visitors attend?

Yes. You are welcome to come, listen and participate at a pace that feels appropriate to you.

What should I wear?

Come in clothing that is comfortable and appropriate for a shared public gathering. There is no expectation to perform an image.

Are children’s arrangements available?

Please contact us before your visit about children’s arrangements, safeguarding and what support may be available for your family.

Do I need to register?

Do not assume registration is required unless the team confirms it for a specific gathering.
