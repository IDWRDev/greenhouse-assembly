A people, not a programme

Everyone can grow.
Everyone can contribute.

Ministry is not reserved for a visible few. It is the shared work of becoming mature in Christ, caring for people and using our gifts responsibly.

Each confirmed pathway will clearly explain who it serves, what participation involves, how people are supported and what a healthy first step looks like.

The participation journey

From interest to faithful service.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Understand the purpose, people and expectations of a ministry pathway.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Begin with a conversation, not pressure or an automatic commitment.

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Receive orientation, care and any appropriate safeguarding guidance.

  4. 04

    Serve

    Take a clear first responsibility with support and accountability.

  5. 05

    Grow

    Develop character, skill, wisdom and capacity over time.

Healthy participation

People should be served well while they serve.

Confirmed opportunities should have clear expectations, appropriate preparation, named support and sensible safeguarding. Interest never guarantees placement, and no one should be pressured to take responsibility before a healthy process is complete.

What to expect
  • A real conversation
  • Clear role expectations
  • Appropriate orientation
  • Support and accountability
