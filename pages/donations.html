Why we give

Gratitude that becomes
faithful action.

Christian generosity begins with gratitude to God. It trains the heart away from fear and self-importance, and toward trust, responsibility and care for others.

We want every giving experience to be voluntary, secure, clearly explained and handled with appropriate accountability.

Our stewardship commitments

Trust deserves responsible systems.

01

No pressure

Giving should never be manipulated through fear, shame or inflated promises.

02

Secure methods

Funds should be sent only through approved, tested and clearly identified channels.

03

Clear purpose

Public giving language should explain its intended ministry context without unsupported claims.

04

Wise care

Personal banking passwords, card PINs and sensitive credentials should never be requested.
