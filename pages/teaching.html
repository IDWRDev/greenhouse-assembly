How we learn

Formation, not information alone.

01

Read deeply

Attend to Scripture patiently, honestly and within the larger story of God.

02

Name what is operating

Learn to notice the beliefs, desires, fears and habits that shape visible choices before they become automatic authority.

03

Practise faithfully

Turn understanding into prayer, character, habits, service and wise action. Grace does not leave truth outside the person.

04

Carry life

Bring mature faith into family, work, leadership, creativity and public responsibility without losing communion with God.
